To build my understanding of Arduino and how to use programming along with it, I completed all 24 lessons included in the Arduino Super Starter Kit over March. Instead of rushing through the activities, I worked on them bit by bit each day so I could fully understand the concepts being taught. Through these lessons, I learned the basics of circuits, LEDs, resistors, sensors, wiring, and coding using the Arduino IDE. Completing lessons helped me improve my problem-solving skills, patience, and ability to troubleshoot errors (A lot of them, as the kit instructions even had flaws that had to be fixed by researching and talking with others in the field) independently while building a strong foundation for more advanced Arduino projects in the future.

Arduino Notes

Throughout the 24 Arduino Super Starter Kit lessons, I made notes for each activity to help reinforce my learning. After completing each lesson, I wrote down the key concepts, wiring steps, code explanations, and any issues. This helped me better understand how each component works and made it easier to remember important ideas such as how circuits are built, how sensors respond, and how code controls hardware. Taking structured notes for every lesson improved my organization skills and allowed me to reflect on my progress as I gradually built a strong foundation in Arduino.

Creative Project 1 - Arc Reactor

My first creativity project was an LED light sequencing system controlled by code, where I attempted to design it as an arc reactor inspired by Iron Man. I arranged multiple LEDs in a circular pattern inside a cardboard structure and programmed them to light up in sequences using Arduino. The goal was to create a visually appealing, rotating light effect similar to an arc reactor design. However, the final result did not match my original vision, as the physical structure was less circular than expected and did not look like an arc reactor. Despite this, the project helped me learn important skills in LED control, timing in code, and how design limitations can affect the final outcome of a build. It also taught me how to improve planning and precision in future creative projects.

Creative Project 2 - Phantom Thieves Calling Card

My second creativity project was a Phantom Thieves-inspired calling card display that focused on text and sound rather than lighting. I programmed an Arduino system that displayed themed text on a screen and paired it with a buzzer that produced a dramatic sound effect when the message appeared. The goal was to recreate the suspenseful calling card moment from Persona 5 in a simple electronic format. I worked on coordinating the timing between the text display and the buzzer so that both elements triggered together for maximum impact. This project helped me understand how to synchronize output components like displays and buzzers, and improved my coding skills in timing and sequencing events in Arduino projects.

Creative Project 3 - Iron Man Helmet

My third creativity project was an Iron Man inspired mechanical helmet design where I attempted to use a servo motor to lift the faceplate. I attached a string mechanism to the servo with ducttape having the idea that it would pull the helmet face upward smoothly, similar to the opening motion of Iron Man's suit. Inspired by a Instagram reel I saw using servos to make cardboard fingers go up and down. However, the project did not fully work as planned because the helmet structure ended up being heavier than the servo motors lifting capacity. As a result, the servo was unable to consistently raise the faceplate. Even though not successful in its final mechanical goal, the project helped me understand limitations, mechanical load balancing, and the importance of matching hardware strength to design requirements. It also taught me how to improve future prototypes by testing weight and resistance earlier in the design process.

Workout Journey

During my CAS activity experience, I focused on improving my physical health and consistency through regular dumbbell workouts. I used dumbbells as my main form of resistance training and tracked my progress over time to build strength, endurance, and discipline. However, I found it challenging to stay consistent due to other life responsibilities and time constraints, such as getting sick way more than ever this year and a sudden family emergency, which disrupted my workout schedule. Because of this inconsistency, I was not able to fully reach my goal of progressing to a 25lb dumbbell, even though it was realistically achievable with consistent training. I got up to 15 lb after starting at 10lb. Despite not reaching that target, the experience taught me the importance of routine, commitment, and adapting goals based on real-life circumstances, while still making meaningful progress in my fitness journey. Helping me with future activites and learning the difficulty people put themselves through to remain consistent

Service

Service Activity 1

My first service experience was through the Social Justice Club, where I aimed to take part in activities focused on raising awareness about important community and global issues. However, my involvement was limited because the club was temporarily discontinued during the first semester due to disruptions following the teacher strike and barely any meetings and volunteer opportunities that followed. Despite this, I was still able to participate in key events once activities resumed. I took part in MMWS2 (Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women and Girls awareness initiative), where I learned about the ongoing impact of violence against Indigenous women and the importance of remembrance and advocacy. I also participated in Pink Shirt Day, which focused on anti-bullying awareness and promoting kindness and inclusion in schools. These experiences helped me understand the value of standing up for social justice issues even when opportunities are limited, and showed me how small actions and participation in awareness events can still contribute to meaningful change in the community.

My second service experience was with the “All The Same” initiative, where I created digital cards using Canva to highlight and support people from minority cultures. The purpose of this project was to promote inclusion, awareness, and respect for cultural diversity by recognizing different backgrounds and identities in a positive way. It helped me realize how digital media can be used as a tool to spread awareness and encourage acceptance in a simple but meaningful way.

My third service experience in the “All The Same” initiative focused solely on writing research-based responses to structured prompts about minority cultures and representation. I researched different cultural groups and social issues related to inclusion, then used this information to write clear and thoughtful answers that explained the importance of diversity and equality in society. CAS Reflection & Conclusion

my CAS experience helped me grow across creativity, activity, and service by pushing me to try new skills, stay consistent, and reflect on my progress. In Creativity, I developed Arduino-based projects that combined coding, electronics, and design, where I learned through both successful builds and failures that helped me improve my problem-solving and technical understanding. In activity, I focused on maintaining a regular dumbbell workout routine to improve my strength and discipline, although I struggled with consistency due to time and life commitments, which prevented me from fully reaching my 25lb goal. In service, I participated in the Social Justice Club, awareness events like MMWS2 and Pink Shirt Day, and contributed to the “All The Same” initiative through both design and written research, which strengthened my understanding of inclusion and social issues. In summary, CAS helped me become more reflective, responsible, and aware of both my personal growth and my impact on the community.
